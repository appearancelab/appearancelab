Dr Nihan Alp is an Assistant Professor of Psychology at Sabancı University, Türkiye, where she leads the Alp Visual Neuroscience (AlViN) Lab. She received her PhD from KU Leuven, was a visiting scholar at Stanford University, and spent a sabbatical at York University in Canada. Her research examines how the visual system integrates information across space and time to construct coherent perceptual experiences. Combining psychophysics with EEG and frequency tagging, she investigates visual organization, dynamic face perception, and the relationship between neural integration and conscious perception.
